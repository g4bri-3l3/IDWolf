The Active Directory domain test.com currently has an extremely critical security posture with a hygiene score of 16/100. The most severe exposure is the presence of multiple non-default user accounts (TEST\lab.svcsync, TEST\gladi.simona, and TEST\aprilette.madelyn) that possess DCSync rights, allowing them to extract all domain password hashes at will. Additionally, dangerous delegation configurations (such as lab.delegsvc with protocol transition and lab-unconstr$ with unconstrained delegation) provide immediate paths to domain controller compromise. A complex web of delegated permissions among non-privileged groups further exacerbates the risk, allowing lateral movement and escalation to Tier 0. Immediate remediation of these structural flaws is required to prevent complete domain takeover.
