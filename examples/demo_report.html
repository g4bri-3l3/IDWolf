How the score is computed

Score = 100 × e−penalty/100, where penalty sums a weight per triggered check at its worst severity (Critical 20, High 8, Medium 3, Low 1), capped by the worst severity present: any Critical check limits the score to 35 (grade E), any High to 65 (grade C). It never reaches zero, so a bad environment and a catastrophic one still rank differently. Per-object counts are shown separately so that many low-impact objects do not hide a single critical exposure.
