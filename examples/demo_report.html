🤖AI summary and remediation plan

The Active Directory domain moosebank.local is in an extremely vulnerable state with a hygiene score of 1/100, presenting widespread exposure to immediate domain takeover. Critical administrative boundaries have collapsed due to unprivileged users possessing direct write access over Tier 0 objects, Group Policy Objects linked to Domain Controllers, and Active Directory Certificate Services (AD CS). Furthermore, multiple privileged accounts are exposed to credential harvesting via Kerberoasting, AS-REP roasting, cleartext storage, and DCSync rights granted to standard identities. Fundamental domain controller defenses are disabled, including PrintNightmare restrictions, Netlogon channel enforcement, and SMB/LDAP signing. Remediation must immediately prioritize revoking unauthorized Tier 0 ACLs, unpublishing compromised certificate templates, and securing privileged credential material.
